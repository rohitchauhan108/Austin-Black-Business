About Made Media Group & Austin Black Business Journal

Informing, Empowering, and Transforming African American Enterprise in Central Texas.

Austin Black Business Journal & Community News magazine is published by Made Media Group, a certified 501(c)(3) non-profit organization dedicated to economic equity, civic education, and leadership recognition. We chronicle the innovators, visionaries, and community pillars defining Central Texas’s economic landscape.

Our Purpose

The Mission of Made Media Group

African American business owners and professionals have shaped Central Texas since the founding of Austin’s historic freedom colonies. Yet their stories, economic achievements, and policy challenges often remain underreported in mainstream media.

Our mission is clear: to illuminate economic opportunities, hold municipal and corporate institutions accountable for equitable supplier diversity, and preserve the generational legacy of Black wealth in Austin, Round Rock, Pflugerville, Manor, and beyond.

Core Beliefs

Our Editorial Standards

  • Uncompromising Accuracy: Independent fact-checking, sourced reporting, and transparent journalistic ethics.
  • Economic Advocacy: Examining municipal procurement, venture funding gaps, and commercial tenancy protections.
  • Community Uplift: Centering living role models across medicine, law, engineering, education, and culinary arts.
  • Cultural Preservation: Chronicling East Austin’s cultural heritage districts and historical landmarks.
What We Do

Our Journey

A Decade of Chronicling Central Texas Excellence

2011

Foundation of Black Austin®

Anita C. Roberts retired from military service and launched Black Austin® to provide an economic forum and resource directory for local entrepreneurs.

2015

Launch of Journal Publication

Austin Black Business Journal debuted in digital and print formats to provide in-depth journalistic analysis of Black corporate and community leadership.

2018

Achievements in Black Austin Gala

Inaugurated the annual gala recognizing Top Black Doctors, Influential Lawyers, and Entrepreneurs, becoming one of Central Texas’s most anticipated civic gatherings.

2022

Silicon Hills Tech & Innovation Focus

Expanded specialized coverage chronicling Black founders, venture funding benchmarks, and STEM initiatives across the Austin technology boom.

Present

Empowering Central Texas

Continuing to bridge the racial wealth divide through impactful reporting, directory advocacy, and Made Media Group community empowerment programs.
